Privacy Policy

Feedmute does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking code.

What happens on your device

  • Safari applies the hiding rules itself. Content blockers are designed by Apple so that the app never sees the pages you visit.
  • Your choices and the compiled rules are stored only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app’s own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network requests

The only network request the app makes is downloading the public list of site rules from this website (woscxy.github.io, hosted by GitHub), at most twice a day. The request carries no identifier. Like any website, GitHub can see your IP address; GitHub’s privacy statement applies.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply. The app’s Report a Site button opens an email that you can read and edit before sending; it contains the app and iOS versions, the rules version and which switches are on.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: cxy130434@gmail.com

デバイス上での処理

  • ページを非表示にするルールを適用するのは Safari 自身です。Apple のコンテンツブロッカーの設計により、アプリがあなたの閲覧ページを見ることはありません。

メール

お問い合わせのメールは、返信のためだけに使用します。アプリの「サイトを報告」ボタンで作成されるメールは送信前に確認・編集でき、アプリと iOS のバージョン、ルールのバージョン、オンになっているスイッチが含まれます。
